 Stochastic Text
 
 A "Stochastic" text might show a lack of awareness of its context in several ways:

1. Inconsistent tone: A stochastic text may switch between different tones, such as formal and informal, without a clear reason or awareness of the reader or purpose.
    
2. Lack of cohesion: The text may jump between different ideas or topics without a clear connection or transition, indicating a lack of understanding of the context and the relationships between the different parts of the text.
    
3. Inappropriate language: A stochastic text may use language that is not suitable for the context, such as using overly technical language in a simple explanation or using simplistic language in a complex discussion.
    
4. Failure to adapt to the readership: The text may not take into account the needs, interests, or level of understanding of the audience, resulting in a lack of engagement or confusion.
    
5. Inability to recognise ambiguity: A stochastic text may not be able to recognise and address ambiguities or uncertainties in the context, leading to unclear or misleading information.
    
6. Lack of situational awareness: The text may not be aware of the social, cultural, or historical context in which it is being used, leading to insensitive or inappropriate language or examples.
