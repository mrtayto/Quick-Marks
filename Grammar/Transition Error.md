Transition Error / Cohesion Error A transition error or cohesion error occurs when the connection between sentences or ideas is unclear or illogical, disrupting the flow of the text. This type of error often happens when transitional words or phrases (like "in addition," "however," "therefore," etc.) are used inappropriately or without a clear link to the preceding or following sentences. As a result, the relationship between ideas becomes confusing, making it difficult for the reader to follow the argument or narrative.

Transition Error: The inappropriate use of transitional words or phrases that fail to clearly or logically connect ideas

Cohesion Error: A broader issue where sentences or ideas do not flow smoothly, often due to poor transitions, resulting in a disjointed or confusing text.

e.g. Transition Error: "The weather was cold. However, I forgot my coat."
